# Photo albums

One folder per album: `YYYY-MM-<place or occasion>/`.

- The folder's `index.md` is its landing page: a title, a date and a few lines
  about the album. Images in the same folder form its gallery, so they need no
  links in the text; link an image only to place it inside the story.
- Import photos as media with descriptive logical names, and describe each
  important image in its link text for readers who cannot see it.
- Leave location metadata and faces of people who did not agree out of anything
  headed for publication.
- To publish, move the whole folder to `public/albums/`.
